GUIDED PROCEDURE

Verify roll, pitch, and yaw

Record guided positions up to ±180° and create a complete 3D orientation file.

Ready to begin.

How to perform the test

Begin with the quad level. Slowly follow each indicated position and hold it until the next stage. The procedure tests ±90° and ±180° on roll, pitch, and yaw, followed by a combined rotation.

The file records gyroscope, accelerometer, quaternion, and displayed angles at 100 Hz.

The quad must be disarmed and the motor test must be disabled.

STATIONARY CALIBRATION

Measure gyroscope bias and drift

Keep the board completely still. The test calibrates the gyroscope, waits for stabilization, then records a diagnostic log.

Ready to begin.

PID / MIXER

Simulated PID and mixer test

Check gyroscope and radio commands while calculating M1–M4 with physical outputs locked at zero.

Ready to begin.

How the simulated test advances

Each stage starts with a three-second preparation countdown. There is no time limit for performing the requested movement.

After the movement, keep the quad still or return the stick to center for just over one second. Only then will the next stage begin.
